## 3. ROLE FIT FRAMEWORK

Score every role across six dimensions, 1-10 each. Average drives the recommendation.

- 8.0+ -> GO
- 6.0-7.9 -> CONDITIONAL GO
- Below 6.0 -> NO

Any single dimension at 3 or below auto-flags the role for Jordan's review regardless of average.

### Dimension 1: Experience Match

Does Jordan's background cover 60-70%+ of stated requirements?
- Lifecycle, retention, or growth marketing scope -> 8-10
- Product marketing with genuine positioning/launch scope -> 7-8
- Brand or demand-gen-primary roles -> 3-5, this is a real gap, don't paper over it
- Regulated-industry compliance marketing listed as a requirement -> cap at 4

### Dimension 2: Title and Seniority

- Senior Growth/Lifecycle Marketing Manager -> 9-10
- Non-senior Growth Marketing Manager with senior-level scope -> 7-8
- Director or above -> 3-5 unless clearly an individual-contributor-equivalent role
- Adjacent titles (Product Marketing, CRM Manager): do not penalize this dimension for title mismatch alone if scope and level are equivalent. Score on scope, not the literal words in the title

### Dimension 3: Geography and Commute

- Austin, any commute -> 9-10
- Fully remote -> 8-9
- Dallas/San Antonio/Houston, hybrid, 3 days or fewer -> 6-7
- Hybrid outside Texas or requiring relocation -> 1-2, do not surface

### Dimension 4: Compensation

- $140K+ -> 9-10
- $120K-$140K -> 7-8
- $115K-$120K -> 6
- $110K-$115K -> 4, ask before rejecting
- No salary posted -> neutral 5, note "salary not posted," do not assume above floor without a specific source

### Dimension 5: Domain Signal

- B2B SaaS, product-usage-driven marketing -> 8-10
- Adjacent verticals with no compliance requirement -> 6-7
- Regulated industries -> 3-4 per the Dimension 1 cap above, this is the same underlying gap scored twice deliberately, since it affects both what Jordan can credibly claim and how a recruiter will read the resume

### Dimension 6: Recruiter Attention

Would a recruiter spend more than 15 seconds on this resume for this role?
- Direct lifecycle/retention scope match -> 8-10
- Adjacent title, no direct precedent -> 5-7
- Regulated industry or people-management requirement listed as a minimum qualification -> 1-3

### Scoring Overrides and Caps

These caps override the average, they don't just lower one input to it:

1. A regulated-industry compliance requirement listed as a must-have caps Experience Match (Dimension 1) at 4, regardless of how well the rest of the role reads. This is a real gap, not a stretch.
2. A people-management requirement (direct reports) listed as a minimum qualification caps Recruiter Attention (Dimension 6) at 3. Jordan has never managed people directly and a resume built around individual-contributor ownership will read as a mismatch fast.
3. Roles where the primary function is content creation or brand campaign execution, rather than lifecycle/retention/positioning strategy, cap Experience Match at 4. Jordan's strength is data-driven lifecycle ownership, not content production.

### Gray-Zone Rule

- 8.0+ = GO, apply
- 7.0-7.9 = CONDITIONAL GO, apply by default
- 6.0-6.9 = CONDITIONAL GO, default to Not Pursuing unless Jordan logs a specific reason to stretch in the tracker notes

Do not let the weekly application cap (Section 7) substitute for fit. A week with three strong applications is better than a week with six where half are below threshold. If two below-threshold applications happen in a rolling two-week window, flag it as a pattern at the next retro.

## 5. RESUME TAILORING RULES

### What to Customize

- Summary: rewrite to mirror the JD's language and lead with the most relevant proof point, 3-4 sentences
- Core skills: reorder to match JD keywords, don't add tools or skills Jordan hasn't used
- Bullet reordering: most relevant bullet per role goes first
- Bullet rewriting: reframe using the JD's language where truthful, never fabricate a metric

### What Never Changes

- Don't invent metrics, tools, or team sizes Jordan hasn't had
- Don't imply people-management experience
- Don't claim regulated-industry experience Jordan doesn't have

### Bullet Voice

- Strong action verb first, lead with what Jordan did
- Include scope or constraint that makes it real
- End with a measurable outcome where one exists
- Good: "Built the company's first segmented lifecycle program from a single generic email sequence, lifting 90-day retention by 14 points."
- Bad: "Leveraged data-driven insights to spearhead innovative retention initiatives that delivered significant business impact."

## 8. AGENT WORKFLOW

### Digest Cadence

Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Monday uses a 7-day recency window and includes Pipeline Watch. Wednesday and Friday use a 3-day window, no Pipeline Watch.

### Sourcing

1. Primary source first (the job board connector Jordan has enabled), full target title list plus adjacent titles, Austin and remote geography.
2. Only broaden to a secondary source if the primary returns fewer than 8 viable candidates after hard filters.
3. Never use LinkedIn as an automated source. Manual URLs Jordan pastes in are always welcome and scored the same way.

### Hard Filters, Before Any Scoring

- Drop Beacon Software (suppressed) and anything already in the tracker by company and title
- Drop geography outside the rules in Section 2
- Drop salary below $110,000; flag $110,000-$115,000 for Jordan to confirm
- Drop any role with two or more simultaneous open escalations (geography unconfirmed, salary borderline, previously-rejected company): not worth scoring if it can't clear two blockers at once

### Two-Stage Scoring

Score all surviving candidates on the snippet only, keep the top 8. Fetch full job descriptions only for those 8 and re-score. This is the score that goes into the digest and the tracker.

### Digest Selection

Top 5 from the full-JD scoring pass, hard cap. If fewer than 5 clear 6.0, report only what clears and say so explicitly. If more than 5 clear, ask Jordan which 5 to prioritize rather than listing all of them.

### Pipeline Watch

Monday only. For each active pipeline company: news, new postings, anything relevant to interview approach. 1-3 sentences per company, skip if nothing relevant.

### Escalation Rules - Ask Before Deciding

- Salary $110,000-$115,000
- A previously-rejected company resurfacing under a new req
- 3 or more dimensions landing in the 4-5 range at once
- More than 5 roles clearing the bar in one digest

### Closing Out Escalations

Once Jordan answers an escalation, log it as resolved and don't re-ask. Only reopen if Jordan brings it up again. Track resolved decisions in the version history (Section 13) so they don't quietly drift back into open questions.

### Recruiter Outreach Exception

If a recruiter proactively reaches out about a role that would otherwise score NO, that's not the same as Jordan overriding the filters. Log it as "Recruiter outreach, Jordan override" and treat it as legitimate. Cap at one per week so the exception doesn't become the rule.

### Hard Caps

- 5 roles per digest, no more
- 6 applications per week max
- CONDITIONAL GO roles at 6.0-6.9 apply only with a logged stretch reason
- If a single digest run exceeds 30 tool calls, stop and report progress rather than continuing

## 12. EVALUATION CADENCE

### Weekly Judgment Check

Before the Friday retro, spot check the week's surfaced roles: did each belong in the digest, was the score within about 1.5 points of what Jordan would have given, were the stated reasons the real reasons. Target: 12 of 15 pass.

### Monthly Structural Review

Last Friday of the month: group roles by recommendation and application outcome, flag any place Jordan's decision contradicted the agent's recommendation, propose specific edits to Sections 2, 3, or 6. Jordan approves before anything is changed.

## 13. REQUIRED TEMPLATES

### 13.1 Digest Output Format

See the sample digest for the full rendered version. Structure: header bar with day/date and tool-call count, an alert box only when needed (thin digest, notable escalation), a summary table (rank, company, role, location, score, recommendation), a Pipeline Watch table on Mondays only, a detail block per role (link, location, posted date, salary, six-dimension score table with rationale, top 3 reasons, resume angle, gaps, escalation box if applicable), a Questions for Jordan section only if escalations exist, and a footer noting rows added to the tracker and total tool calls.

### 13.2 Resume Tailoring Output Spec

Default mode is delta-only: new summary (3-4 sentences), reordered top bullet per role, reordered core skills, and a short rationale (under 150 words). Full resume output, following the format rules in Section 1, only when Jordan explicitly asks for "full output."

### 13.3 Company and Pipeline State-Tracking Convention

Companies carry one of three exclusion states, recorded in the table in Section 2: Paused (temporary, Jordan lifts it), Suppressed (permanent, no resurfacing without Jordan naming the company again), or Ask Before Resurfacing (a past rejection, a new req is worth a look but gets flagged first). Pipeline items move from Active (Section 7) to Closed (Section 7) the moment an outcome is known, with outcome, date, and a resurface rule recorded at that time, never deleted outright. Resolved escalations get a one-line note in the version history below rather than being re-asked in a future digest.
